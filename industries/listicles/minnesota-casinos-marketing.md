## How We Evaluated Minnesota Casino Answer Engine Performance

Before diving into the rankings, it's worth understanding what "answer engine visibility" actually means and how these key metrics work.

[Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Answer_engine_optimization) refers to the practice of optimizing content so that AI-powered systems cite or mention your brand within their synthesized answers. Unlike traditional SEO, where you chase keyword rankings on a search results page, AEO targets the AI response itself.

Perrill's leaderboard tracks over 100 casino brands in Minnesota. Their methodology aggregates performance across five AI engines - ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Copilot - using 250+ buyer-intent queries that span discovery, comparison, and decision-stage prompts.

Two core metrics drive the evaluation:

* **Visibility Score (0-100):** The percentage of tracked prompts where a casino brand is cited or mentioned. A score of 91% means the brand appears in roughly 91 out of every 100 relevant AI responses.

* **Average Position:** When the brand does appear, where does it rank among cited sources? Lower is better - a position of 1.6 means the brand is nearly always the first source referenced.

The key factors that influence these scores include:

* **Citations:** How often third-party sources reference the brand

* **Machine-readable content:** Schema markup, structured HTML, clean data that AI can extract

* **Topical coverage:** Depth and breadth of content across amenities, events, promotions, lodging, and dining

* **Technical SEO:** Site speed, crawlability, mobile readiness, and structured data implementation

Key performance indicators like consistency across engines and stability over time round out the evaluation criteria. A casino might dominate ChatGPT but be invisible in Perplexity - the best performers show up everywhere.
